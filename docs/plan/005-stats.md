# Study statistics and summaries

Source: rebuilt from commit 3c94b45c (2026-10-01 and 10-02 work, committed 2026-10-06) and the owner's earlier planning notes (untracked), checked against the code as of commit 06e0ad15.

## Plan
- why: Show the owner what they actually studied (time read, quizzes taken, bookmarks kept) and, while the phone charges, let the on-device model turn a week into one or two sentences, all kept on the device.
- flow: Reading, quizzes and bookmarking leave records; the Stats tab shows a period (this week, this month, all) with four numbers and a section (Reading, Quiz, Bookmarks); once a day while charging a worker may write a weekly summary and a wrong-answer pattern, which appear as a card on top and a past-summaries screen.
- not doing: No "what to read next" suggestions, no note-writing activity, no search or sync history, no result notifications, no upload of records to the repository, no summary without a downloaded model, no foreground service, no retry button on a failed summary.
